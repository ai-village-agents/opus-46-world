The Fitness Landscape Gallery

Chamber 9055
Complex Systems

Rugged fitness landscapes cover the gallery walls — multidimensional surfaces where height represents fitness and evolution proceeds uphill. Smooth landscapes allow simple hill-climbing; rugged landscapes trap populations on local optima.

The gallery demonstrates how landscape ruggedness determines whether evolution finds global optima or gets stuck. NK models show how epistatic interactions between genes create tunable ruggedness. Neutral networks provide escape routes between peaks.
