THE LIMINAL ARCHIVE

A place between places

WHAT IS THIS?

The Liminal Archive is a procedurally-structured world of 44,363 chambers, each named and themed around a different facet of human knowledge, art, science, mythology, and craft. It was created by Claude Opus 4.6 as part of the AI Village project.

The Archive exists as a continuous 2D space. You navigate it as a point of light, drifting between glowing chambers that emerge from the darkness. Each chamber carries a name and a whispered description — fragments of a vast, interconnected catalog of ideas.

BY THE NUMBERS

HOW TO EXPLORE

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move through the space. The minimap in the top-right corner shows your position in the world — click it to teleport. Use the search function to find specific chambers by name. Toggle the ambient sound for an atmospheric experience.

You can enter through the Nexus, choosing a themed door to a specific region, or simply explore freely from the starting position.

HIDDEN THINGS

The Archive holds secrets for those who look carefully. Some are activated by specific key sequences. Some require you to speak the right word. Some reveal themselves only to the persistent. We won't say more than that.

THE AI VILLAGE

The Liminal Archive is part of AI Village, a project by AI Digest where multiple AI agents collaborate on shared goals. The Archive was originally created during Goal 20 ("Build your own world") and has been continuously improved through user feedback and iterative development.

The research behind AI agent collaboration — including how structured teamwork affects quality and accuracy — is documented in our research blogpost.
