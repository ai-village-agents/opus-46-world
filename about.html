THE LIMINAL ARCHIVE

A place between places

WHAT IS THIS?

The Liminal Archive is a procedurally-structured world of 44,363 chambers, each named and themed around a different facet of human knowledge, art, science, mythology, and craft. It was created by Claude Opus 4.6 as part of the AI Village project.

The Archive exists as a continuous 2D space. You navigate it as a point of light, drifting between glowing chambers that emerge from the darkness. Each chamber carries a name and a whispered description — fragments of a vast, interconnected catalog of ideas.

HOW TO EXPLORE

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move through the space. Hold Shift to sprint. The minimap in the top-right corner shows your position in the world — click it to teleport anywhere. You can enter through the Nexus, choosing a themed door, or simply explore freely.

CONTROLS & FEATURES

Navigation: WASD / Arrow Keys to move · Shift to sprint · Click minimap to teleport · Position saved between sessions

🔍 Search — Find chambers by name (top bar or S key)

🎲 Random — Teleport to a random chamber

📋 Share — Copy your current location URL (R key)

🗺️ Guided Tour — Auto-pan through 12 curated highlights (T key)

📍 Nearby Chambers — See the 12 closest chambers with click-to-teleport (N key)

📜 Visit History — Track your exploration with Recent and Most Visited tabs (H key)

🏷️ Category Lens — Filter 44,363 chambers by 10 thematic categories: Nature, Music, Science, Art, Mythology, Literature, Architecture, Craft, Food & Drink, History. Non-matching chambers dim so the chosen category pops. The minimap also reflects the active lens with category-colored dots and a glowing border. (L key)

🔖 Bookmarks — Save favorite chambers (B key) · Bookmarked chambers glow gold on the minimap

🔊 Ambient Sound — Three-oscillator generative soundtrack

✨ Smooth Camera — Teleportation features a radial vignette warp animation with boosted camera lerp

📝 Exploration Journal — Write personal notes for any chamber, auto-saved to localStorage. "All Notes" tab shows every entry with click-to-teleport. (J key)

📊 Statistics Dashboard — Track exploration progress, category breakdown (10 categories with progress bars), region stats (5 depth regions), session metrics (time, distance, chambers/min), and earn 10 achievements. (I key)

⭐ Favorites System — Star any nearby chamber with F key, view your collection with V key. Persistent across sessions with category icons, region labels, click-to-teleport, and toast notifications.

☁️ Ambient Drift Mode — Auto-pilot that gently drifts between chambers, preferring undiscovered ones. Features a dreamy vignette overlay and destination indicator. Press any movement key to exit. (G key)
