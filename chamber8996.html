The Plasmonic Resonance Hall

Chamber 8996
Nanotechnology

Gold and silver nanoparticles fill the hall with impossible colors. Particles smaller than the wavelength of light interact with electromagnetic radiation through localized surface plasmon resonance — collective oscillations of conduction electrons that concentrate light into nanoscale volumes.

The hall demonstrates how shape controls optical response: spheres absorb green light and appear red, while rods can be tuned across the entire visible and near-infrared spectrum. Dimers create hotspots where electromagnetic fields are enhanced a millionfold.
