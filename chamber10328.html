Armor and Vulnerability

Chamber 10328
The Semiotics of Clothing

Armor is the most honest garment: it admits you are afraid, that the world is dangerous.

Paradoxically, admitting vulnerability through armor is a form of strength.

Modern emotional armor works similarly — we dress defensively, projecting toughness to hide tenderness.

But armor that never comes off becomes a prison, protecting you from connection as well as harm.
