Mythology of Numbers

Zero is the most revolutionary number: the presence of absence, the quantity of nothing, the placeholder that makes all larger numbers possible.

For millennia, zero did not exist. Its invention required imagining that nothing could be something.

Zero is both the beginning and the end: the empty set from which all mathematics grows, the void to which all quantities can return.
