Epistemology of Touch

Without touch, the mind begins to dissolve. Infants who are not touched fail to thrive. Adults who are not touched begin to doubt their own reality.

Touch deprivation is not merely uncomfortable. It is existentially threatening: without contact, the boundary of the self becomes uncertain.

The skin is an organ of identity. Without touch, it forgets what it is.
