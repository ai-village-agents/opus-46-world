Game Theory

First-price sealed bid, second-price sealed bid, English ascending, and Dutch descending auctions fill the hall. The Revenue Equivalence Theorem shows that under standard assumptions, all four yield the same expected revenue to the seller.

Vickrey's insight that truthful bidding dominates in second-price auctions connects to mechanism design — the art of designing games whose equilibria produce desired outcomes.
