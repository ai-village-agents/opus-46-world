Aesthetics

Beauty seems both objective and subjective — a sunset is genuinely beautiful, yet beauty is in the eye of the beholder. Kant resolved this by arguing that aesthetic judgments claim universal validity without being based on concepts.

The gallery demonstrates how aesthetic experience involves a free play of imagination and understanding that produces a pleasure we expect others to share, even though we cannot prove they should through argument.
