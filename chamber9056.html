The Attractor Basin

Chamber 9056
Complex Systems

Dynamical systems flow toward attractors — fixed points, limit cycles, and strange attractors that organize the long-term behavior of complex systems. The basin maps the set of initial conditions that flow toward each attractor.

The Lorenz attractor's butterfly wings demonstrate sensitive dependence on initial conditions, while limit cycles show how rhythmic behavior emerges from nonlinear dynamics. Multistability means the same system can exhibit qualitatively different behaviors depending on history.
