The Desert of Boredom

Chamber 10453
Cartography of Emotions

Boredom is a desert: vast, featureless, and secretly full of life for those who learn to look.

Beneath the surface of boredom, the mind is working: sorting, connecting, preparing for the next creative surge.

The desert of boredom is not a wasteland. It is a fallow field. Everything that grows next will grow from here.
