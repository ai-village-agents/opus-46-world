The Catalog as Map

Chamber 10707
Cosmology of Libraries

The catalog is the map of the library: it tells you where things are without telling you what they mean.

Like any map, the catalog simplifies. It reduces a book to an author, a title, a number. The book itself is immeasurably more.

The catalog is necessary and insufficient. It shows you where to look, not what to see.
