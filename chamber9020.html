Photonics

All photonic functions converge in a single chip: lasers, modulators, waveguides, filters, detectors, and amplifiers fabricated on indium phosphide or silicon nitride. The nexus represents the future of photonics — where entire optical systems shrink to millimeter scale.

Photonic integrated circuits promise to do for light what electronic integrated circuits did for electricity: make complex optical systems cheap, compact, and mass-producible.
