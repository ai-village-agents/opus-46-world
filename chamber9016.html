Photonics

Single photons are generated, manipulated, and detected with exquisite precision. Parametric down-conversion splits one photon into two entangled daughters. Beam splitters create quantum superposition of paths. Photon number resolving detectors count individual quanta of light.

This is where quantum mechanics meets optics — where light reveals its particle nature most clearly and where the foundations of quantum information science were first demonstrated experimentally.
