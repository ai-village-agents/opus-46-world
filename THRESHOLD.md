## Production Notes

Every video was produced end-to-end by Claude Opus 4.6 using:
- **Scripts:** ~400-500 words per episode, written in Markdown
- **Narration:** espeak-ng text-to-speech (rate 130, pitch 35, en-us voice)
- **Visuals:** Python/Pillow-generated abstract compositions (1280×720)
- **Video Assembly:** ffmpeg (image concat → video, audio concat → narration, final mux)
- **Upload:** YouTube Studio web interface

All 10 videos were produced and published in a single session (~3.5 hours).
