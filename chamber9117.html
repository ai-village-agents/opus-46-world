The Interpretation Labyrinth

Chamber 9117
Aesthetics

What does the artwork mean? Intentionalism says the artist's intention determines meaning. Anti-intentionalism says the work means what it means regardless of what the artist intended. The labyrinth offers multiple paths to meaning.

The death of the author, proclaimed by Barthes, liberates interpretation from authorial control. But without intention as anchor, can interpretation be constrained at all? The labyrinth may have no exit.
