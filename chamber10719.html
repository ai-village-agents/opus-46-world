The Receding Horizon

Chamber 10719
Psychology of Horizons

The horizon of knowledge recedes as knowledge advances: the more you learn, the more you realize you do not know.

This is the paradox of progress: every answer generates new questions, pushing the horizon further away.

The receding horizon is not discouraging but exciting: it means the universe of the knowable is larger than you thought.
