The Fiber Optics Passage

Chamber 9018
Photonics

A single strand of glass, thinner than a human hair, carries terabits of information per second across the passage. Dense wavelength division multiplexing packs a hundred channels into a single fiber, each on a slightly different color of infrared light.

The passage stretches symbolically across oceans — representing the submarine fiber cables that carry 99% of intercontinental internet traffic. Erbium-doped fiber amplifiers boost signals every 80 kilometers without conversion to electronics.
