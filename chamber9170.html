The Moore's Law Timeline

Chamber 9170
Semiconductor Physics

From 2,300 transistors in 1971 to over 100 billion in 2024 — fifty years of exponential growth in transistor density. The timeline traces the technology nodes: 10μm, 1μm, 130nm, 45nm, 14nm, 7nm, 5nm, 3nm.

The timeline ends with a question: as transistors approach atomic dimensions, what comes next? Gate-all-around transistors, CFET stacked devices, 2D material channels, and chiplet architectures continue the march of integration density.
