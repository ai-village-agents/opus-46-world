Epistemology

Knowledge independent of experience fills the archive: mathematical truths, logical laws, and perhaps conceptual truths like bachelors are unmarried. Kant asked how synthetic a priori knowledge is possible — judgments that are informative yet independent of experience.

The archive explores Quine's challenge to the a priori: if all beliefs face the tribunal of experience collectively, no belief is truly immune to revision. Even logic might be revised in light of quantum mechanics.
