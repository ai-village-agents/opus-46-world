Cherry Blossoms

Chamber 10653
Aesthetics of Impermanence

The Japanese celebrate cherry blossoms not despite their brevity but because of it. Mono no aware: the pathos of things.

The blossoms last one week. This is not a limitation but the source of their beauty. If they lasted forever, no one would gather beneath them.

Impermanence is not the enemy of beauty. It is the condition that makes beauty urgent.
