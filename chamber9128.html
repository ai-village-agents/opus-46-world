The Non-Monotonic Reasoning Room

Chamber 9128
Logic

Classical logic is monotonic: adding premises never invalidates conclusions. But common sense reasoning is non-monotonic: learning that Tweety is a penguin defeats the default inference that Tweety flies.

The room demonstrates default logic, circumscription, and autoepistemic logic — formalisms that capture defeasible reasoning, closed-world assumptions, and the revision of beliefs in light of new information.
