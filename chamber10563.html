The Weather of Dust

Chamber 10563
Cosmology of Small Things

Dust has its own weather: it rises and falls, drifts and settles, forms clouds and storms at the scale of the domestic.

A sunbeam through a window reveals the atmosphere of the small: particles tumbling in currents too slight for human skin to feel.

The weather of dust follows the same physics as the weather of clouds. Scale is the only difference.
