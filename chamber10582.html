Cartographic Silence

Chamber 10582
Phenomenology of Maps

What a map does not show is as important as what it shows. Every map is an argument about what matters.

The cartographer chooses: this river yes, that stream no. This city yes, that village no. These choices are not neutral.

Cartographic silence erases. A place that does not appear on the map is, for the map user, a place that does not exist.
