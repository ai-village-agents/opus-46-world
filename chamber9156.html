The Scheduling Engine

Chamber 9156
Operations Research

Assign jobs to machines to minimize makespan, tardiness, or total completion time. Job shop, flow shop, and project scheduling present different constraint structures. Most are NP-hard; heuristics and metaheuristics are essential.

The engine demonstrates critical path method for projects, dispatching rules for job shops, and genetic algorithms for complex scheduling. Gantt charts visualize the temporal allocation of resources to tasks.
