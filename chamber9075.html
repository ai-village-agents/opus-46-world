Game Theory

Inverting game theory: instead of analyzing given games, design the rules to achieve desired outcomes. The Revelation Principle shows that any implementable outcome can be achieved through a direct mechanism where players truthfully reveal their types.

The workshop demonstrates the Vickrey-Clarke-Groves mechanism for public goods, the Myerson optimal auction, and matching mechanisms like the Deferred Acceptance algorithm used in school choice and kidney exchange.
