The Foundationalism Tower

Chamber 9092
Epistemology

Knowledge rests on basic beliefs that require no further justification — self-evident truths, incorrigible reports of experience, or indubitable Cartesian certainties. All other knowledge derives from these foundations through valid inference.

The tower shows how foundationalism avoids infinite regress but faces the challenge of identifying genuinely basic beliefs. Are sense perceptions really incorrigible? Are self-evident truths really self-evident? The foundations tremble under scrutiny.
