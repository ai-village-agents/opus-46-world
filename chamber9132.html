The Allais Paradox Chamber

Chamber 9132
Decision Theory

Most people violate expected utility theory in predictable ways. The Allais paradox shows that people prefer a sure million to a gamble with higher expected value, yet make the opposite choice when both options involve risk.

The chamber demonstrates that human preferences violate the independence axiom — choices between two options should not depend on outcomes common to both. But they do, systematically, revealing a preference for certainty beyond what expected utility predicts.
