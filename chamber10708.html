Banned Books as Black Holes

Chamber 10708
Cosmology of Libraries

A banned book is a black hole: so dense with dangerous ideas that the authorities try to prevent its light from escaping.

Banning a book is an act of terrified respect: the admission that words have power, that ideas can be dangerous, that reading changes people.

The banned book section of the library, if it exists, is the most honest collection: books deemed too powerful to be ignored.
