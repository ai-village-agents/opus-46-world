Epistemology

Knowledge is not just individual but collective. Scientific communities, democratic institutions, and epistemic networks produce and distribute knowledge through social processes that can amplify or distort individual cognition.

The forum explores testimony networks, epistemic injustice, groupthink, wisdom of crowds, and the social organization of science. Individual rationality is necessary but insufficient — the social structure of inquiry matters.
