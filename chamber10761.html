Philosophy of Repair

In kintsugi, broken pottery is repaired with gold. The crack is not hidden but highlighted. The repair is the art.

Kintsugi philosophy says: the history of breakage and repair is part of the object, not something to conceal.

The golden seam is more beautiful than the unbroken surface. The repair adds value that the original did not have.
