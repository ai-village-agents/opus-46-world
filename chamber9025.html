The Symmetric Key Forge

Chamber 9025
Cryptography

AES — the Advanced Encryption Standard — dominates the forge. Its elegant substitution-permutation network processes 128-bit blocks through 10 rounds of SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, and AddRoundKey operations.

The forge demonstrates how a single shared secret key enables both encryption and decryption. Block cipher modes of operation — ECB, CBC, CTR, GCM — show how block ciphers extend to encrypt messages of arbitrary length.
