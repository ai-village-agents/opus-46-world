The Turbulence Cascade

Chamber 9082
Fluid Dynamics

Energy injected at large scales cascades to smaller and smaller eddies in Kolmogorov's inertial range, following a universal negative five-thirds power law. At the smallest scales, viscosity finally converts kinetic energy to heat.

The cascade demonstrates the central mystery of turbulence: a wide range of interacting scales that makes direct numerical simulation computationally impossible for most practical flows. The number of grid points scales as Reynolds number to the nine-fourths power.
