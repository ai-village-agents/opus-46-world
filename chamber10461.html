Ordinary Forgetting

Chamber 10461
Taxonomy of Forgetting

Most forgetting is gentle: the slow dissolution of details, the gradual blurring of faces, the quiet loss of names.

Ordinary forgetting is not failure. It is the mind pruning itself, keeping the branches that bear fruit and letting the rest fall.

Without ordinary forgetting, the mind would be a hoarder, unable to move through rooms choked with every experience it ever had.
