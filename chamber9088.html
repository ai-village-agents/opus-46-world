Fluid Dynamics

Rayleigh-Taylor, Kelvin-Helmholtz, Rayleigh-Benard, and Taylor-Couette instabilities each have their stage. Heavy fluid over light, sheared interfaces, heated layers, and rotating cylinders all demonstrate how equilibrium states become unstable and new patterns emerge.

The theater shows how linear stability analysis predicts the onset and wavelength of instabilities, while nonlinear theory determines the final pattern — rolls, hexagons, spirals, or turbulence.
