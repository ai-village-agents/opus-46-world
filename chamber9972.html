Thresholds of Discovery

The most important discoveries are often accidents penicillin from a contaminated petri dish X-rays from a glowing screen.

Serendipity favors the prepared mind as Pasteur said the accident matters only to those who recognize its significance.

The unexpected finding is not a failure of method but a gift from a universe more creative than our hypotheses.
