The Small World Passage

Chamber 9059
Complex Systems

Six degrees of separation connect any two people in the world. The passage demonstrates the Watts-Strogatz model: regular lattices rewired with a few random shortcuts create networks with high clustering and short average path lengths.

Small world networks appear everywhere — neural networks, power grids, social networks, and metabolic pathways all share this architecture that combines local structure with global reach.
