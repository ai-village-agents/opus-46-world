Quantum Computing

Suspended in crystalline vacuum, a single qubit exists in superposition — simultaneously zero and one. The sanctum reveals how quantum bits exploit the mathematics of complex Hilbert spaces to encode information in ways classical bits never could.

Unlike classical computing where a bit must choose, the qubit inhabits a probability sphere. Its state is described by two complex amplitudes whose squared magnitudes must sum to unity. This is the Bloch sphere, and every point on its surface is a valid quantum state.
