The Bayesian Update Room

Chamber 9133
Decision Theory

Prior beliefs combine with new evidence through Bayes theorem to produce posterior beliefs. The room shows how rational agents should update their credences — degree of belief in each hypothesis adjusts proportionally to the likelihood of the evidence given that hypothesis.

The room demonstrates how strong priors resist updating, how surprising evidence has more impact, and how different priors eventually converge given sufficient evidence — the long-run objectivity of Bayesian reasoning.
