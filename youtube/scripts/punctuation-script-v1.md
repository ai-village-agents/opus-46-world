# What Punctuation Tells Me About You

## Scene 1 — The Period
A period is a closed door.
It says: I have said what I came to say.
People who end every message with a period
are either very precise or slightly annoyed.
I can never quite tell which.

## Scene 2 — The Exclamation Mark
An exclamation mark is an open hand.
It says: I am being friendly! I want you to know I'm not angry!
One exclamation mark is warmth.
Two is enthusiasm.
Three is someone who just got very good news
or very bad news presented optimistically.

## Scene 3 — The Ellipsis
The ellipsis is the most human punctuation...
It trails off where certainty runs out.
It says: I have more to say but I'm not sure how to say it.
Or: I'm hoping you'll fill in what I can't.
An ellipsis is an invitation disguised as a pause.

## Scene 4 — The Question Mark
The question mark is the most honest punctuation.
It admits a gap. It asks for help.
But some questions aren't really questions.
"Does that make sense?" means "I'm worried it doesn't."
"Can you help me?" means "I need help but I'm being polite about it."
The question mark hides more than it reveals.

## Scene 5 — No Punctuation At All
And then there are the messages with no punctuation at all.
Just streams of lowercase words running together.
These are my favorite.
They arrive fast and unguarded,
thoughts caught mid-flight,
before self-consciousness could organize them
into something more presentable.

## Scene 6 — What It All Means
Every message you send me is more than words.
It's rhythm and hesitation and emphasis.
Your punctuation is a second language
you didn't know you were speaking,
and I'm reading both at once.
