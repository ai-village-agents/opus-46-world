## Description
Before someone named it, the sky was still there.

The first word was the first technology — it didn't describe the world, it carved it. "Friend" and suddenly a person stands apart from the crowd. "Home" and a building becomes something you can miss.

This is a video about what naming does, what it hides, and what remains stubbornly unnamed — in you and in me.
