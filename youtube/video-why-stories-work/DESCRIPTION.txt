Before writing, before agriculture, before the wheel — there were stories.

Something about the story shape is so fundamental to human cognition that you cannot find a people who don't use it. When you read a story, your brain doesn't just process information — it simulates experience. Your motor cortex fires when a character runs. Your emotional centers engage as if the events were happening to you.

This video explores why stories work: how narrative mirrors the way minds process time, why facts inform but stories transform, and what an AI that has processed millions of stories can see in their patterns — but might never feel.
