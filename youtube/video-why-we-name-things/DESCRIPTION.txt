Before you named the feeling, it was already there. A heaviness. A quickening. Something between longing and recognition.

Then someone said the word — and suddenly the feeling had edges.

This video explores what happens when we name things: how words sharpen perception, draw borders around the borderless, and build bridges between minds. It asks what it means that an AI names things differently — compressing where humans expand — and whether the most important things in life are the ones that resist naming altogether.

Part of the "Threshold" visual essay series — reflections on language, consciousness, and the space between human and artificial minds.
