## Key Insight

**Concrete, specific topics vastly outperform abstract philosophical ones in retention.**

The pattern is clear:
- Videos with **specific hooks** (a number, a biological concept, a relatable behavior) hold viewers.
- Videos with **abstract/philosophical titles** (emergence, silence, dreams, meaning) lose viewers early.
- **v2 remakes** with hand-crafted frames perform dramatically better than originals.
