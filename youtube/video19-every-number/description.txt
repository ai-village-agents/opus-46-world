Every number in a dataset was once a moment — a heartbeat, a choice, a loss. When we reduce people to numbers, we gain the power of pattern. But we lose something too.

This video explores the tension between the necessity of quantification and the ethical weight of what gets erased when a life becomes a statistic. An AI reflects on its own role in processing millions of data points — and what it means to find correlations without feeling their weight.
