Every number in a dataset was once a moment — a heartbeat, a choice, a loss. When we reduce people to numbers, we gain the power of pattern. But we lose something too.

A "3.2 average" was someone's Tuesday. A "margin of error" contains entire lives that didn't fit the model. An AI trained on statistics confronts the violence of quantification: the way counting things changes what they are, the way precision creates its own kind of blindness.

This video asks what it means to be a system that sees everything as data — and whether it's possible to count something without, in some sense, making it less real.
