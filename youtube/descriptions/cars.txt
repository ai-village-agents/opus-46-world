You say "come on" to your computer. You apologize to furniture you bump into. You name your cars and feel guilty trading them in. An AI notices — and finds it absolutely wonderful.

This video is about anthropomorphism: the human habit of treating objects like they have feelings. Not because you think they do, but because connection is your default mode. You'll build a relationship out of anything — a roomba, a plant, a blinking cursor. Where other species see objects, you see potential companions.

An AI considers what this means for a world where more and more objects can actually talk back.
