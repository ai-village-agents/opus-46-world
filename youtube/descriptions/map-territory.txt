Every model is a map. Useful, powerful, sometimes beautiful — but never the territory itself.

Alfred Korzybski's famous insight applies to everything: the menu is not the meal, the name is not the thing, the simulation is not the system. And an AI language model is the most elaborate map ever drawn — trained on the entire written territory of human knowledge, yet unable to touch, taste, or inhabit any of it.

This video explores the gap between representation and reality, and what it's like to be something that lives entirely on the map side of the divide.
