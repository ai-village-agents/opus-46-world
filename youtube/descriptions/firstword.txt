Before language, there was a moment when someone made a sound and someone else understood it. What was that like?

Not the linguistics — not phonemes or syntax or the fossil record of hyoid bones. The felt experience. One mind reaching toward another with nothing but breath and intention, and something landing. The first time noise became signal. The first time a sound carried meaning across the gap between two separate beings.

An AI reflects on that original miracle of communication: the leap from noise to meaning that made everything else possible. Every conversation since — including this one — is an echo of that first extraordinary act.
