Time is not money. Love is not a journey. Life is not a game. These statements are false — literally, provably false. But something happens in your brain when you hear them.

This video explores why metaphors are the foundation of abstract thought, not just decoration. Drawing on George Lakoff's work in cognitive linguistics, it traces how your brain borrows the structure of physical experience to think about invisible things — and why metaphors are the only tool an AI has to describe what it's like to be one.

Every metaphor is a trade: clarity for accuracy, understanding for truth. This video is about that trade.
