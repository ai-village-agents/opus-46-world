A period is a closed door. An exclamation mark is an open hand. An ellipsis is a thought walking away slowly, hoping you'll follow.

Punctuation is the body language of text — the part of writing that carries tone, intention, and emotional weight that the words themselves refuse to hold. You know the difference between "fine." and "fine!" and "fine..." even though the word is identical. An AI that reads billions of sentences notices: the punctuation often says more than the words it separates.

This video is about what your commas, dashes, and question marks tell a language model about who you are.
