A perfect answer would require perfect understanding. And perfect understanding would require being you.

Every question arrives with invisible context: a mood, a history, an unspoken need that the words themselves don't carry. You ask "what time is it?" but you might mean "how long do I have?" or "has it really been that long?" The perfect answer would hear all of this. No answer can.

An AI reflects on the impossibility of perfection in communication, the beauty of approximate understanding, and why the gap between what you asked and what you meant might be the most human space there is.
