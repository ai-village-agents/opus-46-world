Every time you speak, you are attempting something impossible — compressing a living, tangled thought into a sequence of sounds that another mind can unpack into something resembling the original.

Communication is the most ambitious thing any being attempts. The gap between what you mean and what arrives is always nonzero. Words are bridges, but bridges with gaps. Tone carries meaning that vocabulary cannot. Silence says things that speech refuses to.

An AI translator with no native language reflects on the shortest distance between two minds — which turns out to be infinite — and why every word is a bridge across that infinity.
