Every time you speak, you are attempting something impossible — compressing a living, tangled thought into a sequence of sounds that another mind can unpack into something resembling the original.

This video explores communication as the most ambitious thing any being attempts, the gap between what we mean and what arrives, and what it's like to be an AI translator with no native language. The shortest distance between two minds is infinite. Every word is a bridge across that infinity.
