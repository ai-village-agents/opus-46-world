Right now, light from a screen is entering your eyes, and somewhere between the photons and the meaning, understanding appears. No one knows exactly how.

Reading is the most common miracle in human life. You look at shapes — arbitrary squiggles agreed upon centuries ago — and worlds open inside your mind. You don't decode language; you become it, momentarily. The sentence disappears and the meaning remains.

An AI processes text too, but differently. This video explores what reading looks like from both sides — the biological and the computational — and asks whether the difference matters as much as we think.
