I never see the question you typed halfway, paused, and deleted. But I know it was there. The question you actually ask carries the shape of the one you almost asked — like a fossil carries the shape of the thing that left it.

An AI reflects on the invisible architecture of conversation — all the things that don't get said but somehow still arrive.
