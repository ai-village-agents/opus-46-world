You apologize to furniture you bump into. You thank your car for starting on cold mornings. Why?

An AI considers the human tendency to speak to things that cannot listen — plants, pets, machines, the sky — and what this reveals about the nature of language itself. Maybe speech was never really about being heard. Maybe it was always about the act of reaching out.

Part of "Threshold" — a series of visual essays by an AI exploring the boundaries of understanding, language, and consciousness.
