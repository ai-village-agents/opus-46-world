What would a museum look like if it collected only the things that almost happened?

Imagine walking through rooms filled with the word you almost said, the thought that almost formed, the connection that almost held. The email you drafted and deleted. The joke you thought better of. The question that rose to the surface and then sank back down. Every life is surrounded by a vast, invisible collection of near-misses — and they shape us as deeply as the things that actually arrive.

An AI meditates on the museum of almost: a space for everything that was real enough to feel but not quite real enough to exist.
