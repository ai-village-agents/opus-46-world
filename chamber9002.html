The Entanglement Loom

Chamber 9002
Quantum Computing

Pairs of qubits hang in correlated stillness, their fates woven together across arbitrary distance. Measure one and the other instantly reflects the result — not through signaling but through shared quantum state.

Einstein called it spooky action at a distance, but entanglement is subtler than telepathy. No information travels faster than light. The correlations were established when the particles last interacted, carried forward like synchronized clocks set together then separated.
